# Hello! Welcome to my page 👋

I'm Huang Frédéric, an apprentice **AI Engineer at SLB's AI Lab** since 2024 and a fifth-year **Master's student in AI & Big Data at ESGI**. LLMs, computer vision, reinforcement learning, and the web to make it all usable. I live in the **Paris, France** region, nice to meet you!

🤵 My portfolio has a guide: **[Luther](https://l-u-t-h-e-r.vercel.app/?ref=github)**, an AI butler who presents my work by voice or in writing, in French, English and Chinese. He can even give you a [two-minute guided tour](https://l-u-t-h-e-r.vercel.app/en/tour?ref=github).<br>
🔭 Right now I'm rebuilding the online shop of my family's jewelry wholesale business on PrestaShop 9. Lately I also shipped [S.I.L.K](https://github.com/Huang-Frederic/S.I.L.K), a Wikipedia race against an AI spider ([play it in your browser](https://huang-frederic.github.io/S.I.L.K/)), and [I.R.I.S](https://github.com/Huang-Frederic/I.R.I.S), the app behind my Pokémon card collection.<br>
🌱 I'm currently learning the **Blackboard pattern** applied to LLM architectures.<br>
💼 Open to work from **September 2027**: AI Engineer, Data Engineer or Data Scientist, in the Paris region or on a VIE abroad.<br>
🌍 I speak French, English, Chinese and the Wenzhou dialect.<br>
💬 Feel free to contact me with any opportunities or suggestions for improvement.<br>
